MailMaster - SaaS Email Marketing Application
Overview
MailMaster is a multi-tenant SaaS application designed for email marketing and campaign management. It allows users to create, manage, and track email campaigns, mailing lists, and templates while supporting multiple tenants with isolated data.

Key Features
User Management: Role-based access control (admin, editor, viewer) with personal and company data.

Mailing Lists: Create and manage mailing lists with tags, categories, and status tracking.

Email Contacts: Store and manage email contacts with custom fields and subscription status.

Email Templates: Design and manage reusable email templates with HTML and plain text versions.

Campaign Planning: Schedule and track email campaigns with detailed analytics.

Multi-Tenant Architecture: Supports multiple tenants with isolated data and domain management.

Reporting & Analytics: Track campaign performance with metrics like opens, clicks, bounces, and engagement scores.
